# MDR150 data radio and HaMDR

Status (2026-10-08): **the emulator runs the newest ham firmware.**
moppe-emu has a CPU16 core, the 68HC16Z1 modules and the MDR150 board
(`emu/notes/mdr150.md`). It runs OH5NXO's HaMDR 174 from his open
bootstrap, and these work in it:
- the console;
- `perm` saving the configuration to flash;
- APRS out: the beacon is decoded from the PWM TX audio;
- APRS in: AFSK into the receiver is decoded by HaMDR's own modem;
- digipeating (WIDE1-1 traced as `OH3RDX*`);
- KISS both ways.

`make -C emu test-mdr150` runs these as scenarios. `python3 emu/python/mdr150tui.py --preset digi --flash radio.bin` gives an interactive radio: serial console, radio state, AX.25 log, Ctrl-A/Ctrl-B to put APRS packets on the air towards it (`emu/notes/mdr150.md`). HaMDR 174 also
rebuilds byte-identical from source: `tools/hc16/hamdr.sh` (below).

## What the radio is

- **Maker:** the type plate (`reference/mdr150/kuvat/outside/mdr_tyyppikilpi.jpg`)
  reads "MDR Systems, Comarco Finland, Mobinet Data Radio, Model MDR 150 ...
  Manufactured for Comarco Finland by 3i Security International srl, Made
  in Italy". The logic-board schematics have a **TELEMIC** title block
  ("MDR-150 1.1", doc 101E 06 001 00, 25.11.94). **Not a Nokia/Mobira
  product.** It was used on Tele/Sonera's 150 MHz Mobinet network and handed
  out to Finnish ham clubs (VAPEPA) around 2004.
- 5 W VHF, 68HC16Z1 CPU, 6 LEDs, a D25F connector with two serial ports
  multiplexed onto one SCI, an unfitted D9 for audio. No display or keypad
  (HaMDR can drive an add-on I2C LCD and keypad).
- HaMDR modes: APRS tracker, KISS TNC, digipeater, and voice radio (the
  last is marked as being removed).

## Hardware (what an emulator needs)

From the schematics, `boot.s`, `pll_etc.s` and the README:

- **CPU:** MC68HC16Z1. 32.768 kHz crystal, SYNCR = 7Fxx gives 16.777 MHz
  (`boot.s`). Peripherals used: SIM (chip selects, PIT, watchdog, port C/F),
  GPT (PWMA = TX audio DAC, IC/OC = handshake lines), QSM (SCI, port QS),
  ADC (RX audio, RSSI, BUSY, UNLOCK). BDM is on test connector JB1.
- **Memory:** 128 KB Am29F010 flash, 8-bit on D8..D15. OE = CSBOOT,
  WE = CS10, CE = A18; it sits at 0x20000..0x3FFFF. 2 x 32 KB SRAM (CS0/CS1
  write strobes, CS2 output enable) at 0x00000, plus the 1 KB internal RAM.
  HaMDR saves its configuration by programming the flash itself
  (`flash.s`), so the emulator needs the AMD command set.
- **Flash map (HaMDR):** 20000 loader (16 KB, never rewritten), 24000
  `.init` (vector image for the loader) `.text2` `.rodata`, 28000
  configurations, 2C000 initialised-data image, 30000 `.text` (64 KB).
- **Radio module:** bit-banged on the IRQ pins (port F): PLL_STB, PLL_DATA,
  PLL_CLK and SR_STB, plus POWL/POWM/POWEL (power level, 4094 output
  enable). The PLL is an **MB1504** with a 12.8 MHz reference
  (`pll_etc.s`). The 4094 switches RX/TX supply, PLL/VCO power, audio amp
  and mute (README).
- **Modem:** TX audio is a PWM sine (GPT PWMA plus an RC filter); RX AFSK
  is demodulated in software from ADC samples (`afsk.s`). Unlike the R58,
  there is no modem chip to model, so the emulator has to feed ADC samples
  at the right times.
- **Serial:** SCI through a 4053 mux (SSEL) to two RS-232 port sets on the
  D25. Updates and configuration always go through port 1 at 19200 8N1.
- **LEDs:** port C (FC0..FC2, ADDR19..21), buzzer on ADDR22, through a
  ULN2003.

Not exactly pinned down: the ADC channel-to-signal order (the schematic
labels are offset) and the RF module's analog behaviour. `afsk.s` and
`hamdr.c` settle the channel use.

## Resident loader

- **Behaviour:** "MDR" typed at 19200 baud within 5 s of power-on enters the
  loader. It takes S-records with a leading `L`, prompts `What (LRSQ)?`,
  and `Q` exits. It cannot be rewritten over serial. On boot it jumps
  through the PK:PC/SP image at 0x24000 (`boot.s`).
- **Dump coverage:** `Ld` dumps 0x20000..0x200A1 (vectors) and
  0x21000..0x219B2 (code and strings). Code is complete (0x21000..0x21714
  in `Ld.s`, with raw bytes in `Ld.traps`). Small gaps in the string table
  are printed there as text, so they can be reconstructed.
- **Emulator options:** rebuild a loader binary from `Ld`/`Ld.s` and boot it
  for real, or start HaMDR directly at the 0x24000 vector with the SIM state
  the loader leaves (SYPCR written and locked, CSBOOT set, RAM at 0).
  0x200A2..0x20FFF is not in the dump; it is probably erased.
- **Missing:** the original Comarco application firmware. HaMDR does not
  need it.

## HaMDR build (verified 2026-10-08)

**Scripted:**
- **`tools/hc16/build.sh`:** builds the toolchain into
  `reference/toolchain/hc16/` from OH5NXO's trees, about 1 minute,
  applying the fixes below.
- **`tools/hc16/hamdr.sh`:** builds HaMDR into `build/hamdr/` and
  checks `hamdr.hex` against OH5NXO's. It also writes `hamdr.map` and
  `hamdr.sym` (2945 symbols, local labels included; for the emulator's
  `load_syms()`). The regenerated `help.s` (from `prconfig.c`) matches
  his too.
- **`tools/hc16/hamdr_syms.py`:** the symbol extractor.

The register-level spec of the chip's modules is in
`notes/reference/hc16z1-peripherals.md`.

The binutils and gcc were rebuilt from `MDR150/gnu/` in the session
scratchpad, as a 32-bit host build. Every HaMDR 174 object and the linked
`hamdr.hex` came out **byte-identical** to OH5NXO's 2015 build: 25 asm
objects, 9 C objects with `-pipe -O -ffreestanding`, and the link with
`hamdr.ldscript`. Fixes needed (now in `tools/hc16/build.sh`):

- **Host:** configure with `CC="gcc -m32 -std=gnu89 -fcommon -w"` and
  `--host=--build=i686-pc-linux-gnu --target=hc16-coff`. The old
  config.sub does not know x86_64.
- **binutils 2.9.1:**
  - delete the stale in-tree `config.status`, `Makefile`, `config.cache` and
    `config.h` files from the source copy;
  - generate `libiberty/lconfig.h` by hand (`NEED_sys_nerr`,
    `NEED_sys_errlist`, `NEED_sys_siglist`, `NEED_sys_nsig`,
    `NEED_strsignal`) and `touch libiberty/errors`;
  - uncomment `static int sys_nerr;` and `static int sys_nsig;` in
    libiberty `strerror.c` and `strsignal.c`;
  - stub `bfd/doc/Makefile`;
  - then run `make all-gas all-ld all-binutils`.
- **gcc 2.8.1:** the archived tree is trimmed and lacks `config/i386`.
  Copy it from upstream gcc-2.8.1.tar.gz (ftp.gnu.org/gnu/gcc/). Then
  `make LANGUAGES=c cc1 cpp xgcc` and compile with
  `xgcc -B<builddir>/`, with gas symlinked as `as` in that directory.
- **Makefile:** HaMDR's `Makefile` is BSD make (`${.IMPSRC}`). `help.s` and
  `help.h` are generated by `prconfig.c`, built with the host cc. The
  flashable file is `loader.hdr` + `hamdr.hex` = `hamdr.out`.

## Versions and flash use (2026-10-08)

Measured from the S-records (used bytes per 16 KB flash sector) and the
strings in each image:

| Image | `.init`.. at 24000 | data image at 2C000 | `.text` at 30000 (64 KB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 162 (2007-01-07) | 10384 | 10160 | ends 3FE77, 392 B free |
| 165 (2008-09-21, last public) | 10530 | 10358 | ends 3FFD1, **46 B free** |
| 174 (2012-09, OH5NXO's tree) | 11072 | 8630 | ends 3CEC2, 12.3 KB free |

- **165 was full.** The "no room for new features" remark fits 165:
  `.text` is 46 bytes short of the end of flash. (The README's own
  2005-12-14 "about 256 bytes left" was about the data image and was fixed
  in January 2006 by moving it to 2C000.) The 174 linker script still has
  no overflow check.
- **174 descends from 162, not from 165.** The public 162 binary equals the
  `hamdr.old/162` tree's `hamdr.hex`. 174's `hamdr_cfg.h` is that tree's
  plus edits, and no source in the archive mentions what 163..165 added:
  - `telemetry_interval` (163), `maxdigi` replacing `hsmaxdigi`;
  - received positions as NMEA waypoints in `gpwpl`/`tll` formats (163;
    162 only had "reports to gps wpts"), `waypoint_talker` (164).
  163..165 were released by Heikki Hannikainen OH7LZB, not OH5NXO
  (changelog: https://wiki.sral.fi/wiki/Hamdr_ohjelmistoversiot; 165 fixes
  TNC2-format printing of mic-e packets, prints own packets in tracker
  mode, drops a leading space from the comment field). The source was in
  OH3GNU's subversion, `http://repo.ham.fi/websvn/ham/hamdr/`. Checked
  2026-10-08, it is lost online:
  - `repo.ham.fi` no longer resolves;
  - its robots.txt was `Disallow: /`, so the Wayback Machine holds only
    the front page and `svn/aprx`;
  - no copy on GitHub (hessu's repos) or Software Heritage.
  The only remaining route is asking OH7LZB or OH3GNU.
- **174 removed** (2009 "undef WX / undef OW", `*_RETIRED` config fields):
  the weather station (anemometer, rain gauge, wind vane, `wx_dump`), the
  1-wire sensors, `txlimit`, the morse `manipulator`, the chatboard, the LCD
  menu's CQ/query/send entries, `i2c_scan`, `omnidf`.
- **174 added:** `squelch_rssi` (noise squelch, renamed from `squelch`),
  RSSI in dB (`dB_zero`, `dB_scale`), `data_limit` (unkey/rekey after N ms
  of AFSK), the watchdog. The Voice and Scanner personalities are still in
  174.
- Config compatibility: 174 appends fields after offset 1546 and renames
  others in place; whether a 165 `perm` block loads cleanly in 174 is
  untested.

## Boot path in the emulator

The emulator doesn't use the Comarco loader. OH5NXO's `bootstrap`
(`hamdr/bootstrap.s`, 2009-12-06, 1518 bytes, rebuilds byte-identical)
replaces it in the flash boot sector:
- it sets up the clock, the watchdog and the chip selects;
- it moves RAM to 0 and flash to 0x20000;
- with DTRA asserted it loads S-records over the SCI and programs the
  Am29F010;
- otherwise it jumps through the vector image at 0x24000 into HaMDR.

The emulator's flash image is this bootstrap plus `hamdr.hex`
(`emu/tests/mdr150/roms.py`). A real radio still carries the Comarco
loader: HaMDR goes in through it (`MDR` + S-records), and the bootstrap
only replaces it if someone programs the boot sector.

## Emulator findings

- **ADC timing:** a 10-bit conversion is 20 ADC clocks in multichannel
  scan, not the manual's 18. OH5NXO measured this on the radio, and his
  demodulator is tuned to the resulting 8066 Hz audio rate. The
  emulator uses 20.
- **Chip-select byte lanes:** on 16-bit ports, BYTE = 01 ("lower")
  strobes the odd byte (D0..D7). CS0 with BYTE 01 is the write strobe of
  the RAM on D0..D7, and HaMDR runs, which confirms it.
- **DTR selects the mode:** HaMDR reads DTR on port 1 when it starts.
  - With DTR, or with defaulted parameters, it runs its command mode
    (echo, `hamdr $`) and the radio does not operate.
  - Without DTR the personality runs, and the console executes commands
    without echo or prompt.
  - This matches the programming-cable instructions in `hamdr.html`.
- **Digipeating range:** HaMDR digipeats only stations within
  `digi_area` (default 10 km) of its own `gps_lat`/`gps_lon`. A packet
  from farther away is printed but not repeated.
- **Interrupts:** HaMDR's modem runs on the GPT OC2 interrupt (vector
  $45, 8192 Hz RX / 16384 Hz TX). The demodulator itself runs from the
  1 kHz PIT on the MAC unit (MAC with modulo addressing, 11 taps per
  filter). The SCI interrupt is vector $50.

## Open questions

- The ADC channel map, confirmed from `afsk.s` and `hamdr.c`.
- Schematic details the emulator assumes: DATA0 pulled low at reset
  (8-bit boot port; R6/R7 on DATA0/DATA9); the RX audio level and the
  RSSI voltage range from the PTV-01; the PWMA RC filter (TX deviation).
- Real-hardware check: none of our builds have been on a radio. The
  verified 174 image is OH5NXO's own build, and it is unknown whether it
  ever ran on a radio. The public 165 is the last released version.
